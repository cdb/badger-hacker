Tilt Dice

A tiny box of gravity. Your photos, on a roll.
LOCAL ONLY / NO BADGE CONNECTION

Arrow keys tilt   Space toss   R reset

Which way is down?

Drag the dot to tilt your imaginary badge. The die falls toward that edge, bumps the walls, and settles.

Six sides. Any pictures.

Choose 1 to 6 images. Fewer than six repeat around the cube.
Photos are center-cropped to 48x48 and saved locally into this app.

Ready with colorful starter faces. Nothing is sent to an external service.

Uses the badge app's Python physics. Browser drawing is an approximation, not a firmware emulator.
